3. Visit the University of British Columbia First Nations and Indigenous Studies Department's Indigenous Foundations website to learn about  the histories, politics, and cultures of the Indigenous peoples of Canada.

4. Read the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada's 94 Calls to Action
