ABOUT THE QUIZZES (10 @ 4%) 

At the end of every Module students will complete a quiz that assesses knowledge of the Module content. Students must complete the quiz in order to gain access to the next Module and associated evaluations. For example, if you do not complete the Module 1 quiz you will not be able to access Module 2, and so on and so forth.

You can complete the Module quizzes at your own pace but please ensure that you keep up with the Course Schedule 

You will be given 12 minutes to complete each quiz. You have only one opportunity to complete each quiz so please ensure that you have adequately covered all Module content, including all parts of each video lecture, case studies and required readings, before attempting the quiz.

This is an individual assignment. Students are NOT permitted to work together to complete the quizzes OR use any generative AI programs, like ChatGPT, for any part of their assignment. 

Value: 10 @ 4%.
