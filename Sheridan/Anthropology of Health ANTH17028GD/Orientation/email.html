SENDING AN EMAIL

  1. Click the Message Alerts button (  ) in the SLATE navigation bar at the top of the page. Two options appear, Go to Pager and Go to Email.
  2. Click Go to Email. Your SLATE Inbox appears.
  3. Click Compose. The Compose New Message dialog box pops up.
  4. You have two choices:
    1. You can input the email address of your intended recipient directly into the To: box, or
    2. Click Address Book. The Address Book pops up. Note that you can filter the available recipients using the Filter By: drop-down arrow to narrow the choices to a single course, for instance.
      1. Select the checkbox next to your intended recipient. You can select multiple checkboxes.
      2. Click one of the To, Cc, or Bcc links () to populate your email.
      3. Click Add Recipients at the bottom of the window. The Address Book dialog box closes.
  5. Complete composing your email by filling in the Subject line and body of your email.
  6. Click Send. Then close the Compose New Message dialog box.

PLEASE NOTE:  I will respond to all emails within 48 hrs during regular business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm).  If you have not heard from me by then please resend your message.
