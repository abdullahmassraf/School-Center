ABOUT THE PALEOPATHOLOGY GROUP ASSIGNMENT (37%)

You will work in a group to explore how biological anthropologists examine and analyze human skeletal remains in order to obtain information about past health. Since this is an online class and we cannot engage in a hands-on examination of human skeletal remains, so you will use a 3D educational application to examine a human skeleton for indicators of health and disease.  Your group will use knowledge that you have gained in this course to examine a late-medieval era human skeleton from head to toe for evidence of health and disease, and then conduct additional research to help you determine the meaning of these findings in a broader biocultural context.

Academic Integrity: Please ensure that your Paleopathology Group assignment abides by Sheridan’s Academic Integrity Policy and that any information referenced outside of your own experiences and ideas are appropriately cited using APA intext citations and bibliography. Students are NOT permitted to use any generative AI programs, like ChatGPT, for any part of their assignment. 

Individual Submission: Each student will review the Module 5 content and the 3D Human Skeleton App and then individually complete the Paleopathology Prep Scavenger Hunt (5%) which will asses your knowledge human skeletal anatomy and the 3D Human Skeleton app, ensuring each student is prepared to successfully complete the Paleopathology Group assignment. At the end of the assignment, each student will individually and confidentially complete an Individual Peer Evaluation (2%) where you evaluate the contributions of yourself and all group members to the project, as well as your specific contributions to the project. 

Group Submission: The group will collaborate and contribute equally to the development of the Paleopathology assignment. The group will collaborate to construct and complete a Group Contract (5%) t to identify roles, ensure all group members contribute equally to the project, and plan the project process.

Collectively, the group will submit one Paleopathology Group Assignment (25%)  that includes contributions of all group members to the submission folder located under the 'Assessments' tab on the toolbar above. Your Paleopathology Group Assignment will be graded with a rubric (located in the Paleopathology Assignment folder with the assignment instructions), so have a look before you begin your assignment. 

Evaluation: The submission will be evaluated and assigned a grade. Individual grades will reflect and each individual group member will receive an individual grade based on their contributions to the assignment. To encourage and ensure equal contribution by all group members, each group member must complete and submit an individual 'Peer Evaluation'. 

Detailed instructions for this assignment will be provided in Module 5. Students will be assigned to a group for this assignment. This assignment is due at the end of Week 10.

Value: 1 @ 37%.
