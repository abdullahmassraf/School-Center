Welcome!

Hello Students,

Welcome to the Anthropology of Health!  My name is Dr. Jaime Ginter and I will be your professor for the course this term. This is an online asynchronous course, meaning that this course is not assigned to a specific day and time, and we do not meet together virtually in real-time each week. You will be responsible for following the course schedule, and completing all content and evaluations by the assigned deadlines.

In this Orientation Module, I have included a land acknowledgement, key information about the course and evaluations, FAQ's, sending email, netiquette, and academic integrity.  Please familiarize yourself with this information and to review the organization of the course site. Please be aware that you must complete the 'SELF-TEST: Are you ready for distance education' AND the 'Academic Integrity Tutorial and Quiz' before you can move on to Module 1.

Please note that you must use Mozilla Firefox in order to access all components of this site; the content is not fully functional when accessed via Explorer.

The course is organized into a number of different Modules.  In each module you will find the course material and evaluations that correspond to that module. Each Module contains an Introduction. The content for each Module is divided into a series of narrated video lectures. After you have covered all of the content in a Module you must complete a Module Quiz in order to release the next Module. Please ensure that you consult the Checklist to verify that you have completed all of the materials and evaluations in the Module.

During the first week of the course please review the information about the course provided on the Course Homepage (Course Outline, Course Schedule and Assessment Overview), as well as the material in the Orientation Module.

Please note that this is a green course. A textbook is not required for the course. Rather, each week you will be assigned articles to read. Links to these articles will be provided in the relevant module.

It is crucial that you participate regularly to be successful in this course. This means that you:

  • watch the video lectures and complete any accompanying activities and evaluations
  • read the assigned readings
  • participate in discussions
  • complete all Module quizzes
  • complete all assignments, reports and projects

All evaluations are due on Sundays at midnight unless otherwise specified on the Course Schedule (this is irrespective of stat holidays). Consult the Checklist for all due dates. The late penalty is 10% per weekday. Assignments more than 5 days late will receive a failing grade.

I am available online to help you if you have problems related to the course material and content. With email, you can expect an answer from me within 48 hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm). My preferred mode of course correspondence is here through SLATE mail, not through Access Sheridan.

Take a few moments now to familiarize yourself with the course schedule and outline, where you will also find detailed information on the course’s learning outcomes, topical outline, evaluation plan, and when assignments are due.

I hope you enjoy the course!
