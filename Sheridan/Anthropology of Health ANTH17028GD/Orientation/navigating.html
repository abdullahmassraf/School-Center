Navigating the Course

There are many ways to navigate the course. An understanding of these features permit you to move to all other course locations in two clicks or less and use the important navigation features with minimum frustration. You are already in the Content area.

SLATE Navigation Bar

The uppermost, dark-blue bar houses all the features that apply to the SLATE system as a whole: your Home page, your courses, your communication options and your profile options.

From anywhere in the course, you can click on the title of the course you are in to return to the course Home (or move to other courses by clicking the drop-down arrow and selecting a different course):

Tool Navigation

The next navigation bar, in light blue, allows you to navigate through different tools. You are currently in the Content Tool area. You can use that bar to navigate to Discussions, Quizzes, Assignment Dropboxes, etc. If you cannot find a tool on the bar, click the More Tools drop-down arrow and see if the tool is listed there.

Content () contains the educational material for the course broken into modules. These modules can also contain links to the other tools.

Discussions () is used for one-to-group communication. The Discussions tool is a collaboration area to post, read, and reply to messages on different topics, share thoughts about course materials, ask questions, share files, or work with your peers on assignments and homework.

The Email () tool allows you to send email from within Learning Environment. You can also organize received mail using folders and store email addresses using the Address Book.

Both Mail and Discussions are asynchronous meaning that the recipient of the message does not see the message immediately.

The Pager () tool is an online messaging tool for sending text messages to classmates and other members of your Learning Environment. A page is quicker and less formal than an email. Use the pager to quickly see which of your classmates are online, get an answer from a friend, or to send a reminder or notice.

Available under More Tools, the Chat tool is a real-time, text-based collaboration tool. You can use the Chat tool to brainstorm ideas, hold a question and answer period, have a debate or discussion, or organize a remote study group. As opposed to other collaboration tools, such as Discussions, Chat conversations occur in real-time.

Available under More Tools, Online Rooms, using Collaborate, is a special version of Chat that allows for chat along with video and audio conferencing, a whiteboard, and screen-sharing.

Dropbox () is a link to all assignment dropboxes in the course. The Dropbox tool enables you to submit assignments in the Learning Environment, eliminating the need to mail, fax, or email assignments. Simply upload your assignment to the appropriate folder.

Use the Grades () tool to check your grades on assignments and tests. You can see your individual grades and comments. You can also view your final grade when released.

Learning Module Navigation

The course is subdivided into learning modules each of which has a navigation system for moving within the module. You are currently in the Orientation Module.

You can navigate using the Previous and Next buttons.

You can also navigate within a module or between modules by using the cookie trail at the top of the screen. Click on the current module name to navigate within a module. Click Table of Contents to navigate between modules.

Finally, you can expand the side panel to navigate within a module. Expanding the panel allows you to see the content in the module you are currently in. You can also use the navigation at the top of the side panel to navigate between modules. The Previous and Next arrows in the side panel will jump to the previous or next module. The Table of Contents button will take to the Table of Contents allowing you to navigate through any module.

A useful function is the Print function. Using this function results in a printed page without the navigation information and boilerplate. This print function works better than your browser print function which may be confused as to what should be printed.
