Collaborative Research Project (20%)

Purpose:

The Collaborative Research Project (20%) is an opportunity for your group to demonstrate your learning from this semester on your choice of interactive multimedia. 

Course Learning Outcomes:

  1. Analyze argumentative texts.
  2. Evaluate evidence in various academic and non-academic contexts.
  3. Integrate primary and secondary sources in the construction of organized research.
  4. Formulate original and informed ideas about a selection of topics and contemporary issues.
  5. Design arguments based on theoretical models.
  6. Collaborate with group members using effective interpersonal communication techniques.  
  7. Use proper styles of documentation in formal essay contexts. 

Artificial Intelligence Permitted:

This assignment is a BLUE/GREEN assignment, as outlined on the School of Communication and Literary Studies AI Scale.

Instructions:

This final stage involves having your group take the information and feedback from your Group Project Proposal and use the data to create a dynamic multimedia project in the Rogerian Style. 

THE ROGERIAN STYLE

Remember: because this is a wicked problem, there will be at least two sides to the issue.  The Rogerian model explores these two sides in the pursuit of a compromise, which is what your final project should achieve.  

The multimedia format of the deliverable is up to your group, but you should choose a mode that is appropriate for your topic and argument.   The purpose is to make the information you have gathered accessible and interesting for a wide audience of people with basic/general knowledge of your topic. 

Your group should have a clear purpose (inform, call to action, persuade) and aim to secure hypothetical support (emotional, legal, political, financial, etc.) from the audience--your professor and peers.  

Some suggestions for multimedia formats include:

  • A 10-15 minute "Dragons' Den" style pitch that you pre-record to present a solution to your topic
  • A blog (here are some  free platforms) that has at least 5 postings related to your topic
  • A 10-15 minute podcast on your topic
  • An "special issue" e-zine (Canva  is a good site to use) that focuses on your topic
  • A 10-15 minute documentary on your topic
  • A 10-15 minute infomercial  as a call to action for your topic
  • A mobile or desktop app
