Collaborative Research Proposal (15%)

Purpose:

The Collaborative Research Proposal (15%) is designed to help your group to solidify your ideas on how to address the wicked problem that you've chosen.  

Course Learning Outcomes:

  1. Analyze argumentative texts.
  2. Evaluate evidence in various academic and non-academic contexts.
  3. Integrate primary and secondary sources in the construction of organized research.
  4. Formulate original and informed ideas about a selection of topics and contemporary issues.
  5. Design arguments based on theoretical models.
  6. Collaborate with group members using effective interpersonal communication techniques.  
  7. Use proper styles of documentation in formal essay contexts. 

Artificial Intelligence Permitted:

This assignment is a Blue/Green assignment, as outlined on the School of Communication and Literary Studies AI Scale.

Instructions:

Your group will complete a project proposal that includes the following elements.  You may use the following points as headings to organize your ideas:

  1. Group names
  2. Group topic 
  3. Definition & explanation of your wicked problem
  4. Context, scope, and parameters for the project (location, who or what is the focus, etc.)
  5. A brief literature review with appropriate in-text citations in MLA or APA style
    1. What is known currently about the problem.  Why is this a pressing issue?
    2. Who are the stakeholders?  
    3. What is at stake?
      1. What are the larger social/political/legal ramifications, implications, or consequences?
    4. Solutions/ideas that have been tried by other groups
  6. A brief outline of your group's proposal/solution
  7. A reference page formatted in MLA or APA style (but it should be consistent with the in-text citations)
  8. A copy of your Group Contract, attached either at the end or as a separate document
