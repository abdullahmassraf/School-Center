Group Check Point #2 (2%)

Course Learning Outcomes:

  1. Formulate original and informed ideas about a selection of topics and contemporary issues.
  2. Collaborate with group members using effective interpersonal communication techniques.

Purpose:

This check point submission folder is for each group member to upload records/copies of their individual contributions to the group project. 

Evaluation:

This submission is individual. It is Pass/Fail and worth 2%.

You can review the rubric for this assignment here. 

What should I submit?

This assignment should not require any work above and beyond what you have done already for your group project.  The goal here is to check what progress you have made and your contributions to the project (hence a pass/fail check point).  

  • Copies of emails/chats/meetings in which you participated
  • Drafts of your work
  • Recordings of any live group sessions
  • Copies of the updates (comments, revisions, track changes) that you've made in group documents
  • Bullet point lists of work that you have done for your Group Role (Representative, Logistics Coordinator, Record Keeper, Quality Director)
  • Any additional materials that you feel demonstrate your contributions to the group efforts
