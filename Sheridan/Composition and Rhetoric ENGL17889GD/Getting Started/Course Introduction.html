Welcome to ENGL 17889GD: Composition & Rhetoric

Course Description

Composition & Rhetoric is an advanced-level English course which focuses on the art of argument and persuasion.  Students explore the function and strategies of argument through reading, writing, and oral presentations.  In this course, students examine different theoretical models for organizing arguments and presenting evidence, employ primary and secondary sources in research, and construct their own arguments. 

Course Learning Outcomes

  1. Analyze argumentative texts.
  2. Evaluate evidence in various academic and non-academic contexts.
  3. Integrate primary and secondary sources in the constructions of organized research.
  4. Formulate original and informed ideas about a selection of topics and contemporary issues.
  5. Design arguments based on theoretical models.
  6. Collaborate with group members using effective interpersonal communication techniques.  
  7. Use proper styles of documentation in formal essay contexts. 


Module Learning Outcomes

Module Learning Outcomes
Module Learning Outcomes
Module 1: Group Dynamics
  1. Identify methods for establishing successful group dynamics. 
  2. Investigate tools and strategies for navigating different stages of group work. 
Module 2: What is an Argument?
  1. Differentiate rhetorical models based upon rhetorical audience and purpose. 
  2. Distinguish the different parts of logical form. 
  3. Identify thesis statements based upon various theoretical models. 
Module 3: Utilizing Evidence
  1. Locate appropriate research sources. 
  2. Classify different types of research sources. 
  3. Analyze various forms of evidence. 
  4. Document sources according to academic and industry standards. 
Module 4: Media Literacy & Fluency
  1. Examine different types of media sources for credibility and bias. 
  2. Evaluate the use of rhetoric in online sources. 
Module 5: Styles of Composition
  1. Examine various styles of composition. 
  2. Identify defining characteristics of theoretical models. 
Module 6: Effective Presentations
  1. Develop strategies for constructing successful presentations. 
  2. Collaborate with peers to develop strategies for delivering engaging presentations. 
Module 7: Completing Group Work
  1. Reflect upon group dynamics, strengths, and weaknesses. 
  2. Evaluate contributions of self and peers to the group work process.
