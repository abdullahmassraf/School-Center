Dr. Jelena Marelj

Hello there!

Welcome to the course!  My name is Jelena (pronounced "Yelena"), and I'm looking forward to working with you this semester. 

Education

I have been teaching a variety of Communications, General Elective, and Degree Breadth Elective courses at Sheridan College since 2014.  I hold a PhD in English Language and Literature from Queen's University, a Master of Arts in English from McGill, and an Honours BA in English, French, and German from the University of Toronto. 

Research Background

My research is interdisciplinary in nature, and includes Shakespeare and early modern drama, rhetoric, linguistic pragmatics, and the pedagogy of critical thinking. I wrote and published an interdisciplinary book on Shakespeare's dramatic characters (Bloomsbury, 2018), as well as numerous articles for academic journals in the field of early modern studies.

My Life and Hobbies/Interests

I'm married and live with my husband and elementary-aged son in Milton, Ontario. I enjoy reading, learning new languages (I'm currently fluent in four European languages), and staying active with my family, whether it's swimming, hiking, or biking.
