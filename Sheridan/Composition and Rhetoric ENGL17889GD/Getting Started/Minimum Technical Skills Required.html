Required Technical and Digital Literacy Skills

  1. Navigate SLATE to access course materials, submit assignments, and participate in discussions.
  2. Email your instructor and attach files to emails.
  3. Create files using MS Word, MS Excel, and MS PowerPoint.
  4. Upload text, video and audio files to complete assignments. 
  5. Manage and organize your files on your computer. 
  6. Use cloud services like OneDrive to store and share files securely.
  7. Use tools such as MS Teams to attend online lectures and collaborate with your peers online. 
  8. Research information in the library or using the library databases. 
  9. Download and install software and applications.
  10. Know how to troubleshoot or find help to troubleshoot common technical issues such as internet connectivity problems.
