Student Support Services Available at Sheridan 

(The support services are ordered alphabetically.)


Accessible Learning 

  • Accessible Learning: Access academic accommodations (testing, classroom, and work-integrated learning) for suspected or documented disabilities. 

Career Services

  • Career Services: Career Services offers a combination of employer events, resources, a job board, and a team of Career Specialists that work together to support you and give you the tools to effectively look for jobs, and plan your education and career. These services are free to both current students and graduates (Alumni) and are provided in-person and virtually. You can book one-on-one appointments with a Career Counsellor or Employment Consultant through Sheridan Works.

First Year Experience  

  • First-Year Experience: Coordinates information, resources, events, workshops, and programs to assist students in their transition to Sheridan.
  • First-Year Experience Videos and Workshops: This playlist includes recordings of our latest Succeeding at Sheridan Workshops, Supports & Services presentation, and more!
  • First-Year Guide: A guide to your first year at Sheridan with numerous resources to help you start your academic journey.

Health and Wellness Services

  • Health Services: Medical consultation is strictly confidential, and no information is released to anyone without the patient's written authorization unless required by law. 

  • Wellness & Counselling: Professional counsellors provide mental health support through individual appointments, group sessions, and healthy campus initiatives.

Library Support 

  • Library Services: Access support on topics such as research assistance, citation questions, and evaluating sources. 

My OTR

  • myOTR: The Office of the Registrar is your one-stop for student inquiries related to admissions, registration, financial aid and awards, student accounts, and academics.

Student Advisement  

  • Student Advisement: This can be a student's first point of contact for any questions or concerns. Student Success Officers and Advisors are here to help them navigate their programs, connect them with resources to support their success and make the most of the student experience at Sheridan.

  • Scholarships, Awards and Bursaries: Each year, Sheridan awards millions of dollars in scholarships, bursaries and awards to eligible students through the Sheridan Student Assistance Program. External awards from corporate, private and community organizations are also available.

Technical Support

  • Ask a Techie:: Whether you are on-campus or learning remotely, the information on this page is your starting point to discover and access Sheridan's most popular technology resources.

  • SLATE: We have a variety of videos to help you navigate the Sheridan Learning Management System (SLATE). 

Tutoring

  • Academic Skills Hub: Self-directed online modules to support student skill development in areas such as time management, group work, math, professional writing, presenting skills, digital skills, taking notes, academic writing, studying, and test-taking skills.  

  • Tutoring: Connect with academically strong, senior-year students for help in courses at no additional cost to them as a student. Math, Chemistry, Writing, Conversation, and Presentation skills development are also available. 

Sheridan Central
Sheridan Central is the main student services hub. Visit Sheridan Central to explore the services and support available to you at Sheridan to make the most of your experience.
