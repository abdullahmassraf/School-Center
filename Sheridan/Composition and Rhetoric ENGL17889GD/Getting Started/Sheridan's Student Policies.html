Sheridan College's Policies


Academic Integrity Policy 

Academic Integrity Policy: This policy outlines the principles of honesty and ethical behaviour in academic work, prohibiting practices such as plagiarism, cheating, and other forms of academic misconduct.

The School of Communication and Literary Studies at Sheridan College has adopted an Acceptable Use Framework for the incorporation of Artificial Intelligence.  You can review the framework here. 


Academic Accommodation Policy

Academic Accommodation Policy: This policy ensures that students with disabilities receive the necessary accommodations to have equitable access to educational opportunities.


Accessibility Policy

Accessibility Policy: This policy commits to providing accessible learning environments and materials for all students, ensuring compliance with accessibility laws and promoting inclusivity.


Acceptable Use Policy

Acceptable Use Policy: This policy governs the appropriate use of institutional IT resources, detailing prohibited activities and behaviours to ensure the security and integrity of these resources.


Copyright-Protected Work Policy 

Copyright-Protected Work Policy: This policy provides guidelines on the lawful use of copyright-protected materials within the institution, ensuring respect for intellectual property rights and adherence to copyright laws.


Grading Policy 

Grading Policy: This policy defines the standards and procedures for assessing student performance, including grading scales, criteria, and the process for grade appeals.


Privacy Policy  

Privacy Policy: This policy outlines the institution’s commitment to protecting personal information, detailing how data is collected, used, stored, and disclosed in compliance with privacy laws.


Student Code of Conduct 

Student Code of Conduct: This policy establishes the expected standards of behaviour for students, detailing the disciplinary actions for violations to maintain a safe and respectful academic community.
